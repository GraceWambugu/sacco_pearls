# Usability evaluation kit (for objective vii)

Use this with 5 to 10 SACCO members or people thinking of joining a SACCO (purposive sample, Nairobi,
as the proposal says). About 15 to 20 minutes per person. Ask for consent first and do not record
names in the results.

## Before you start (say this)

"This app shows the financial health of deposit-taking SACCOs using figures SASRA publishes. I am
testing the app, not you. Please think aloud as you go. There are no wrong answers."

## Tasks (time each one; note any help you had to give)

| # | Task | Success when the participant... |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | Find Stima DT SACCO and tell me its health rating. | says "Green, 85" (2024) |
| T2 | Which part of Stima's score is weakest, and why? | points to Asset quality (Amber) and mentions non-performing loans |
| T3 | Find two SACCOs in Nairobi and compare them. Which ranks higher? | opens Compare with two Nairobi SACCOs and reads the rank |
| T4 | Why are Protection and Liquidity not scored? | explains that SASRA does not publish the figures |
| T5 | How have Stima's total assets changed since 2020? | opens Trends, chooses Total assets, reads the rise |
| T6 | Save a SACCO so you can find it later. | signs in (or registers) and sees it under Saved |

Record for each task: completed (yes / with help / no), time in seconds, errors, comments.

## System Usability Scale (SUS)

Ask after the tasks. 1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree.

1. I think that I would like to use this app frequently.
2. I found the app unnecessarily complex.
3. I thought the app was easy to use.
4. I think that I would need the support of a technical person to be able to use this app.
5. I found the various functions in this app were well integrated.
6. I thought there was too much inconsistency in this app.
7. I would imagine that most people would learn to use this app very quickly.
8. I found the app very cumbersome to use.
9. I felt very confident using the app.
10. I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with this app.

Scoring: for odd items subtract 1 from the answer; for even items subtract the answer from 5. Add the
ten results and multiply by 2.5 to get a score from 0 to 100. Average across participants.
68 is the usual "average" benchmark.

## Extra questions for this project

- Did the colours and the words Green / Amber / Red make the rating clear? (1 to 5)
- Would this information change which SACCO you join or stay in? Why?
- Was anything confusing or missing?

## Reporting (Chapter 5)

A table of task success rates and average times, the average SUS score with the range, the main
problems people had, and what you changed (or would change) because of them. Prototype findings so
far: Compare was hard to find and the Saved list did not refresh (both fixed: Compare tab, shared
state with provider).
